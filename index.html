How to play · 玩法

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries. Each guess is a column, read top to bottom like traditional Mongolian; columns go left to right. Under each column you see the guess written as a joined word.

After each guess the tiles change colour. In this example the guess is hagan, shown as tiles and, below them, as a joined word:

  • Green: right letter, right position (h).
  • Yellow: in the word, but in another position (g). A letter is yellow only as many times as it occurs in the answer.
  • Grey: not in the word.

Look-alike letters

Some letters share a shape in some positions (o/u, ö/ü, t/d, a/e). Turn on Latin labels in settings to see which letter each tile and key is. Answers never look identical to another dictionary word.

Hints and modes

  • After your 4th guess you can reveal the Chinese meaning (提示).
  • Daily: one word per day, the same for everyone. Practice: unlimited random words.
  • Dimmed keys are loanword letters; they are allowed in guesses but never appear in answers.
  • Word length 4, 5 or 6 can be changed in settings.

Physical keyboard

Type Mongolian directly, or Latin: a e i o u n b p h g m l s t d j y r, c=ch, Shift+O=ö, Shift+U=ü, Shift+N=ng, Shift+S=sh, Enter, Backspace.
